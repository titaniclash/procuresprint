Seven success criteria

Judged against the Phase 1 audit and the evidence in this report.

1 · Usability Met

Labelled navigation, one page pattern per job, every number filters the list below it. Not yet tested with users.

2 · Consistency Met

One shell, one token file, one table, one status map, one AI pattern on all 43 pages.

3 · Accessibility Partly verified

0 automated violations, keyboard and reflow checks pass. Screen-reader pass and AT user testing still to do.

4 · Efficiency Met

Create PR fits one screen per step instead of 2,320 px; work list starts higher with more rows visible.

5 · Visual quality Met

Single brand colour, ruled surfaces, tabular figures; decorative hero and saturated blocks removed.

6 · Responsiveness Met

100% fluid width; 43 pages × 11 widths from 320 to 2560 px with no sideways scroll.

7 · Business alignment Partly verified

Figma flows kept; 6 modules built on assumed scope (Q5) need owner sign-off.

Fixed during Phase 5

Found by the checks above and corrected before handover.

  • Light theme is now the default and ignores the host's dark mode; dark is opt-in with data-ps-theme="dark". MUI theme updated to match.
  • All content containers are 100% fluid; fixed caps on the page (1,600 px), docs (1,280 px) and forms (960 px) removed.
  • Top bar overflowed at 320 px; tightened for phones and kept to one line at tablet widths.
  • Risk meters and bar rows overflowed at 320 px; now shrink.
  • Status chips and controls were hidden behind horizontal scroll on tablets; secondary columns now step aside between 600 and 1023 px.
  • 11 px count badges, keyboard hints and dates raised to the 12 px floor.
  • Create PR step buttons and date inputs had no visible focus; fixed.
  • Approval list truncated text under increased spacing; now wraps.
  • Assistant button lost its name when its label was hidden; given an accessible name.
  • Forced-colours mode lost chip borders and selection; boundaries restored.
  • Wide tables that scroll sideways were not reachable by keyboard at narrow widths; they now take focus with a label when they overflow.
  • Filtering tabs now point to the table they control; scroll padding keeps focused items clear of sticky bars.
